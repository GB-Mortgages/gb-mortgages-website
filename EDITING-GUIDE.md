# Editing your website

## Log in

1. Go to www.gbmortgages.co.nz/admin.
2. Sign in with the email invite you received.

## Change some words

1. Choose **Home page**, then the section you want. The sections are numbered from the top of the page down.
2. Change the text.
3. Click **Publish**, then **Publish now**.
4. Your change is live in about 2 minutes. Refresh the website to see it.

## Headings

Most headings have three parts:

- **Main text**: the first part, in dark green.
- **Accent text**: the second part, in the italic or lighter green style. Leave it empty if you don't want one.
- **Yellow full stop**: tick it to add the saffron full stop at the end.

## Lists, photos and quotes

- **Lists:** use **Add** to add an item, or drag the handle to reorder. Numbers like 01, 02 and 03 update by themselves.
- **Photos:** click the photo, then **Choose different image**, and upload. Use a photo at least 1200 pixels wide.
- **Client quotes:** add, edit or remove them under **8. What clients say**.

## The disclosure statement

1. Choose **Disclosure statement**.
2. Each section has a title and text. Use the toolbar for bold, links and bullet lists.
3. Update **Last updated** whenever you change it.

## Placeholders

Anything in [square brackets] shows highlighted in yellow on the site. Replace these with the real details before launch.

## If something goes wrong

Every change is saved as a version, so nothing is lost. Contact Jolie at Conduct and she can roll the site back.
